What is in one

Posts and site.yml exactly as written, a consistent snapshot of the database, and the uploaded image masters. Not the renditions and not public/ — both are regenerated after a restore, and keeping them would roughly quadruple every archive to protect files that can be made again in seconds.

These are written to the same machine the site runs on, which covers a mistake but not a failed disk. Downloading one puts a copy somewhere else, which is the part a backup on the same drive cannot do for you.
